changelog=
    2.0.0 - Faster viewer, planning tools and exchange formats.
        - Performance: merged building tiles (10k buildings: 44,249 -> 447 draw calls), worker-built geometry, on-demand rendering, BVH picking; 50k-building scenes load about twice as fast.
        - Visual quality: physical sky and sky lighting, 4x MSAA and GTAO, lit windows at night, straight-skeleton hip and gable roofs, glossy glass, optional depth of field and tree wind.
        - Planning tools: GPU sun hours, sky view factor and viewshed; zoning what-if scenarios with existing | A and B | A split views.
        - Exchange and output: 3D Tiles 1.1 and CityJSON 2.0 export, view links, screenshots up to 8K, MP4 tour videos, WebXR, portable packages ready for GitHub/GitLab Pages and Netlify.
        - QGIS: a 3D preview panel with two-way building selection.
        - Exporter: DEM cropped to the scene (tiled DEFLATE GeoTIFF), lighter GeoJSON, unchanged layers kept on re-export.
        - Fix: the sun stood on the opposite side of the sky; zoning envelopes were never drawn.
        - Breaking: English-only UI and attribute names (map Turkish column names in the field mapping); exported data moved to web/data/vector, so re-export existing projects.
    1.0.3 - Redesign plugin icon to high-end 3D tactile brand design with circular teal pedestal, zero margin, and refreshed documentation links.
    PlanX 3D City Viewer 1.0.0 - Flagship Official Release.
        - Includes 1-click OSM Urban Harvester expansion, Atmosphere & Lighting Studio, Visual Readiness Gauge, WebGL Performance Tier Estimator, Volumetric GFA Calculator, GeoTIFF Edge Blending, and Keyboard Shortcuts HUD.
        - Added dynamic 0-100 percent Scene Readiness Score and Pre-Flight WebGL Performance Tier predictor (Ultra 60+ FPS, High Performance, Heavy Urban Scene) to quality report.
    PlanX 3D City Viewer 0.8.66 - Atmosphere & Lighting Studio presets.
        - Added 5 visual lighting presets (Midday Sunlight Clean, Golden Hour Architectural, Overcast Soft Studio, Night Lights & Glow, Morning Mist) to QGIS publisher.
    PlanX 3D City Viewer 0.8.65 - Full 1-click OSM urban harvester expansion.
        - Expanded Overpass harvester to fetch waterlines, streetlights, benches, bus stops, and trash bins in 1 click alongside buildings, roads, greens, and trees.
    PlanX 3D City Viewer 0.8.64 - Nav tab visual icons and geographic CRS reprojection advice.
        - Added visual tab icons (0 Guide, 1 Data, 2 Check, 3 Style, 4 Publish) and automatic UTM reprojection guidance to quality report.
    PlanX 3D City Viewer 0.8.63 - Master AI audit fixes.
        - Cleaned Bandit B110 try/except pass occurrences, added vector layer commitChanges on attribute edits, and preserved road access keywords in presets.
    PlanX 3D City Viewer 0.8.62 - Mapped attribute fields summary in quality report.
        - Quality report displays active attribute column mappings (floors, population, road width, tree height, function).
    PlanX 3D City Viewer 0.8.61 - Auto-detect attributes button.
        - Added a 1-click Auto-detect attributes button to re-scan smart column aliases.
    PlanX 3D City Viewer 0.8.60 - Auto QGIS map canvas extent zoom.
        - QGIS map canvas auto-zooms to imported sample dataset or harvested OSM boundary.
    PlanX 3D City Viewer 0.8.59 - Smart attribute field auto-detection.
        - Automatic pairing for katadedi, nufus, floors, genislik, and function columns.
    PlanX 3D City Viewer 0.8.58 - Hero header quick export button and unneeded root file cleanup.
        - 1-click Export & Open 3D Viewer button in dialog hero header and cleaned residual root files.
