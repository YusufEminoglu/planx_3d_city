about=
    PlanX 3D City Viewer transforms QGIS vector and raster layers into an interactive, browser-based 3D city cockpit powered by Three.js and WebGL. Designed for urban planners, architects, and spatial analysts, it bridges 2D GIS datasets with tangible 3D urban morphology and environmental screening.
    
    KEY CAPABILITIES:
    - Volumetric 3D Massing: Automatic smart massing, podium and tower combinations, stepped high-rises, perimeter courtyard blocks, cantilevers with pilotis, and recessed penthouse crowns configured globally, by zoning function, or via QGIS quick styles.
    - Architectural Facade and Roof Library: High-resolution procedural and photographic facade textures (Residential A-F, Urban F, Curtain Wall, Nordic Timber, Anatolian Brick, Heritage Stone, Industrial Loft, Brutalist Concrete) paired with regional roof presets (Turkish Tile, Copper Patina, Gravel Ballast, Lead Mansard, Flat Terrace).
    - Unified Layer Workflows: Flexible multi-layer staging supporting DEM elevation surfaces, raster plan textures, aerial basemaps, buildings, zoning blocks, cadastral parcels, road networks, tree canopies, and urban street furniture. No mandatory layers required.
    - In-Browser Spatial Analytics: Real-time solar shadow studies with interactive sun clock, daily sun exposure, Sky View Factor (SVF), interactive 360-degree viewsheds, pedestrian wind impact screening, and hypsometric terrain tinting.
    - Presentation and Export Studio: Narrative camera tours with keyframe animation, high-resolution snapshot capture (up to 8K), MP4 cinematic video recording, CityJSON, 3D Tiles, GLTF/GLB models, and standalone offline 3D HTML export packages.
    - Bidirectional QGIS Integration: Live two-way selection synchronization between the browser viewport and the QGIS map canvas, accompanied by attribute inspection and district-scale density statistics.
    
    Runs locally via an embedded lightweight web server (127.0.0.1); no external cloud dependencies or third-party data uploads required. Compatible with QGIS 3.28 through QGIS 4.x. Developed with direct feedback from educational workflows and design studios at Dokuz Eylul University, Department of City and Regional Planning.

    2.2.0 - Realistic streets and street life, dynamic facades, one English material catalogue.
        - Roads: asphalt with aggregate and cracks, lane, edge and centre lines, stop lines and zebra crossings painted in the shader at every junction, all roads in one draw call; honours oneway and lanes. Sidewalks with pavers and a raised curb that open where side streets join.
        - Street life: instanced cars, vans, taxis and buses drive in their lanes, keep their distance and turn at junctions, with headlights at night; instanced pedestrians walk the sidewalks and paths. Only those near the camera and in view are drawn.
        - Buildings: floor slabs follow the volume they belong to, flat roofs carry parapets and rooftop plant, a stone plinth at street level, contact shadows and window blinds by day. New Style toggle: Parapets, Plinths and Rooftop Plant.
        - Dynamic facades (new default, Style > Facades): window bays and floors laid out per wall, never cut by a corner, in six styles chosen from the building's function and height; a planx_facade attribute still wins.

        - English only: Turkish keyword matching is gone from the building code, and the roadmap and reference manual are in English.
